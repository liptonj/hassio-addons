Sign a request

Sign a certificate signing request (CSR) from another system, such as a RADIUS, web, or VPN server, or another CA such as Meraki Systems Manager's SCEP CA. The download holds the signed certificate followed by its CA chain.

Only sign requests from systems you control. Every signing is written to the add-on log.

Other trusted CAs

CA certificates devices must also trust, such as the CA that issued your RADIUS server's certificate for EAP-TLS Wi-Fi. They are added to Apple profiles (and trusted for the Wi-Fi network), to .p12 files, and to ca-bundle.pem.

Download a profile for your MDM

A standard, unsigned Apple configuration profile (.mobileconfig) with the payloads above. Upload it to any MDM as a custom profile; it is unsigned so the MDM can replace device variables and sign it. The macOS profile installs for the whole Mac (System keychain).

Your MDM's variable for a unique value, which it replaces on each device: the serial number for a device certificate ($SERIALNUMBER in Jamf, $DEVICESERIAL in Meraki, $SERIAL_NUMBER in Kandji) or the user name for a user certificate ($USERNAME in Jamf, $OWNERUSERNAME in Meraki). Not needed for Certificates only.

Added to the certificate as an email alternative name, e.g. $EMAIL in Jamf or $OWNEREMAIL in Meraki. The device must have a user assigned in the MDM.

SCEP profiles contain the challenge, so keep them private.
